Advertising Rules

Set the content requirements for advertisements submitted to GLOBAL MARKET.

GLOBAL MARKET media requirement

Every advertisement must support at least 5 videos and photographs uploaded by the advertiser showing their actual products or services.

The platform's intended minimum is five videos per advertisement.
Require Advertiser Photos

Advertisers must provide their own product or service photographs.

Require Five Videos

Advertisements must include at least five video uploads.

Media Review

Submitted photos and videos should be reviewed before publication.

Administrator Approval

New advertisements should wait for administrator approval.

Require Listing Description

Every advertisement should include a clear description.
