1. Introduction

Welcome to GLOBAL MARKET.

This Privacy Policy explains how the platform intends to collect, use, store, share and protect personal information when users access its website and related services.

The policy is intended to apply to buyers, sellers, advertisers, business owners and other individuals who interact with the platform.

GLOBAL MARKET intends to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Kenyan data protection laws, including the Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services used, GLOBAL MARKET may collect the following categories of information.

A. Account Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number, where provided.
  • Account username and authentication information.
  • Account type and preferences.

B. Business Information

  • Business name and description.
  • Business category and location.
  • Business contact details.
  • Business logo and cover images.
  • Other information voluntarily submitted during registration.

C. Advertisement Information

  • Product or service descriptions.
  • Prices and availability information.
  • Uploaded photographs and videos.
  • Advertisement categories and locations.

D. Communication Information

  • Messages sent through platform communication tools.
  • Contact form submissions.
  • Support requests and feedback.

E. Technical Information

  • Browser and device information.
  • Technical logs and diagnostic information.
  • IP address, where collected by platform infrastructure.
  • Website usage and interaction information.

F. Payment-Related Information

If paid services are introduced, relevant transaction references, payment status and limited payment-related information may be processed. Sensitive payment credentials should be handled by the relevant payment provider rather than stored directly by GLOBAL MARKET.

3. How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected through different interactions with the platform, including:

  • Creating or updating an account.
  • Registering a business.
  • Publishing advertisements.
  • Sending messages or contact enquiries.
  • Using platform features and services.
  • Interacting with platform infrastructure and security systems.

Some information is provided directly by users, while certain technical information may be generated automatically by the services used to operate the website.

4. How We May Use Information

Personal information may be used for legitimate and clearly explained purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Displaying business profiles and advertisements.
  • Helping users communicate with one another.
  • Responding to customer support requests.
  • Processing eligible platform service payments.
  • Maintaining platform security.
  • Detecting fraud and misuse.
  • Improving website performance and usability.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

Personal information should only be processed for appropriate purposes and on a valid legal basis under applicable data protection law.

5. How Information May Be Shared

GLOBAL MARKET may share relevant information where necessary to provide its services, comply with the law or protect users.

Other Platform Users

Information deliberately included in public business profiles and advertisements may be visible to visitors. This can include business names, descriptions, product images, public contact details and listing locations.

Service Providers

Information may be processed by authorized technology providers that support hosting, authentication, database services, storage, communications, security or payment processing.

Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed where required by law or through valid legal processes, subject to applicable legal protections.

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The platform or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and understand how services are used.

Depending on implementation, these technologies may:

  • Support essential website functions.
  • Maintain user preferences.
  • Help measure website performance.
  • Support security and fraud prevention.

Where required, users will be provided with appropriate information and choices concerning non-essential cookies and similar technologies.

7. Data Storage and Hosting

GLOBAL MARKET is being developed with a planned backend architecture that may use Supabase services for authentication, database storage and file storage.

If implemented, these services may store account information, advertisements, business records, messages and uploaded media according to the configured access controls and storage arrangements.

The final hosting locations, service providers, database configuration and applicable data transfer arrangements must be confirmed before launch.

8. Data Security

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be implemented to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Planned security measures include:

  • Secure authentication and account controls.
  • Database access restrictions.
  • Appropriate file-storage permissions.
  • Secure communication using HTTPS.
  • Regular review of access permissions.
  • Security monitoring and incident response procedures.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Actual protections will depend on the implemented systems and their ongoing maintenance.

9. Data Retention

Personal information should only be retained for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Retention periods should take account of account activity, transaction records, legal requirements, security needs and applicable dispute-resolution obligations.

The final retention schedule will be established before public launch.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including the right to:

  • Be informed about the processing of personal information.
  • Access personal information held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Object to certain forms of processing.
  • Request restriction of processing where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Exercise applicable rights relating to data portability.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law. Some requests may be subject to legal exceptions or verification requirements.

Users may submit privacy-related enquiries through our Contact Page.

11. Children's Privacy

GLOBAL MARKET is not intended to encourage children to provide personal information without the safeguards required by applicable law.

Where services involve children or individuals who require additional legal protections, appropriate age-related safeguards, parental consent and other legal requirements must be followed.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected unlawfully from a child, appropriate steps will be considered in accordance with applicable law.

12. Third-Party Services and Links

GLOBAL MARKET may contain links to external websites or use third-party services to provide platform functionality.

These services may have their own privacy policies, terms and security arrangements. Users should review the relevant policies before providing information directly to external providers.

GLOBAL MARKET does not automatically control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

13. International Data Transfers

Some technology providers may process or store information in countries outside Kenya.

Where personal information is transferred outside Kenya, the platform must ensure that the transfer complies with applicable Kenyan data protection requirements.

Relevant safeguards and provider arrangements should be confirmed before the platform begins processing personal information through those services.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

GLOBAL MARKET may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in technology, services, legal requirements or data-processing practices.

Revised versions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Where significant changes require additional notice or action, appropriate communication will be provided.
